### WHO WE ARE

AfyaWise Community Based Organization is a grassroots initiative in Meru County empowering healthier communities through local solutions. We focus on maternal and child health, mental health, wellness, Persons Living with Disability and WASH (water, sanitation, and hygiene). Guided by five core goals, AfyaWise expands access to care, builds community capacity, and promotes lasting health practices across Meru and beyond.

## Our Core Values

- Inclusivity: Embracing diversity and ensuring no one is left behind

- Transparency:Operating with openness and accountability

- Community-Centered:Prioritizing the needs of the community in all activities

- Sustainability:Promoting lasting solutions to healthcare challenges

- Collaboration:Building partnerships with stakeholders to maximize impact.

## Our Objectives

- To increase access to quality prenatal, antenatal, and postnatal care services for women of reproductive age in target communities by 40% within two years.

- To reduce the prevalence of common childhood illnesses (e.g., diarrhea, pneumonia, and malnutrition) by 30% through community-based nutrition and immunization programs within 24 months.

- To establish and support at least five community-based mental health support groups and referral systems for early diagnosis and treatment within one year.

- To increase the percentage of households practicing safe water handling, proper sanitation, and handwashing with soap by 50% in the project areas within 18 months.

- To train 100 community health volunteers (CHVs) and local leaders on integrated health promotion strategies for maternal, child, mental health, and WASH by the end of the first project year.
