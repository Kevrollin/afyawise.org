## OUR IMPACT AREAS

Empowering Communities through integrated, sustainable health solutions

![Maternal & Child Health](Icons/maternal.png)

### Maternal & Child Health

We work to improve prenatal, antenatal, and child health outcomes through community education, nutrition programs, immunization campaigns, and safe motherhood initiatives.

![WASH Initiatives](Icons/faucet.png)

### Water, Sanitation, and Hygiene (WASH) Initiatives

Our WASH programs educate households on safe water handling, hygiene, and sanitation while supporting infrastructure like handwashing stations and latrine construction to prevent waterborne diseases.

![Mental Health Support](Icons/mental-health.png)

### Mental Health and Disability Support

We promote mental health awareness, reduce stigma, and establish support groups for mental wellness and children living with disabilities, while training caregivers and community leaders in psychosocial support.

![Community Empowerment](Icons/community-empowerment.png)

### Community Empowerment

We strengthen the capacity of Community Health Volunteers (CHVs), caregivers, and local leaders through structured training, mentorship, and leadership development.

## COMMUNITY IMPACT

AfyaWise has made significant strides in improving health literacy and access to healthcare services in Karama Ward, benefiting hundreds of community members since its inception.

## OUR FUTURE PLANS

Building Healthy Communities, One Step at a Time!

At AfyaWise, we envision a stronger, more resilient healthcare system rooted within the communities we serve. Our future plans are focused on sustainability, innovation, and empowerment:

![EMPTY ALT](Icons/health-center.png)

Community Health Resource & Learning Center

Establish a center for training CHVs, caregivers, and youth, offering counseling, resources, and storage for health supplies.

![EMPTY ALT](Icons/money.svg)

Income Generating Activities for CHVs

Launch small-scale projects like poultry farming, beekeeping, kitchen gardens, and production of sanitary pads to empower CHVs economically.

![EMPTY ALT](Icons/digital-health.png)

Digital Health & Data Innovations

Develop a mobile health reporting app for real-time disease tracking, referrals, and data-driven health planning, even in remote areas.

![EMPTY ALT](Icons/partnership.svg)

Stronger Partnerships with International Organizations

Collaborating with global health organizations

![EMPTY ALT](Icons/ambulance.svg)

Mobile health clinics

Launch mobile health clinics to serve remote areas.

![EMPTY ALT](Icons/expand.png)

Expansion of health education programs

Broaden outreach to schools and the youth with vital health knowledge on prevention, nutrition, and hygiene.
