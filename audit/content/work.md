## OUR WORK

At AfyaWise, our work is dedicated to improving community health, empowering vulnerable populations, and building long term sustainable healthcare solutions. Through a combination of targeted programs, strong partnerships, and community-driven initiatives, we continue to deliver impactful services while planning for even greater expansion in the years ahead.

### Our Current Programs And Activities

![EMPTY ALT](/assets/maternal-program-R5qiwf5m.jpg)

#### Maternal & Child Health

Goal: Increase access to quality prenatal, antenatal, and postnatal care by 40% within 2 years.

Key Activities:

- Conduct public health awareness forums.

- Host health screenings & community wellness days.

- Partner with schools, churches, and local leaders for health education.

- Use radio and public forums for mass sensitization.

![EMPTY ALT](/assets/mental-program-IGm9KoTB.jpg)

#### Mental Health & Disability Inclusion

Goal: Enhance mental health services and support persons living with disabilities.

Key Activities:

- Conduct anti stigma awareness forums.

- Establish community support groups.

- Train caregivers, teachers & CHVs in mental health first aid.

- Build mental health referral pathways.

![EMPTY ALT](/assets/water%20program-Bmtjeyg5.jpg)

#### Water, Sanitation & Hygiene (WASH)

Goal: Strengthen household and school hygiene practices.

Key Activities:

- Conduct hygiene education forums.

- Distribute water treatment supplies.

- Promote safe water storage and sanitation.

- Support handwashing & latrine construction.

![EMPTY ALT](/assets/community%20program-xAujVsxQ.png)

#### Community Wellness & Health Education

Goal: Promote holistic community wellness and preventive healthcare.

Key Activities:

- Conduct public health awareness forums.

- Host health screenings & community wellness days.

- Partner with schools, churches, and local leaders for health education.

- Use radio and public forums for mass sensitization.

![EMPTY ALT](/assets/capacity%20building-BSM2fmEy.png)

#### Capacity Building & CHV Empowerment

Goal: Build capacity of Community Health Volunteers (CHVs) and local leaders.

Key Activities:

- Train CHVs in integrated health promotion.

- Provide reporting tools, kits, and mentorship.

- Conduct regular CHV refresher trainings.

- Implement CHV supervision and recognition systems.

![EMPTY ALT](/assets/partnership%20program-ERZq6bLQ.png)

#### Partnerships & Resource Mobilization

Goal: Strengthen multi stakeholder partnerships to sustain health programs.

Key Activities:

- Engage government health officers and community leadership.

- Partner with NGOs, CBOs, faith based organizations & educational institutions.

- Mobilize technical advisors and donors.

- Form joint coordination committees.

## OUR FUTURE PLANS

Building Healthy Communities, One Step at a Time!

At AfyaWise, we envision a stronger, more resilient healthcare system rooted within the communities we serve. Our future plans are focused on sustainability, innovation, and empowerment:

![EMPTY ALT](Icons/health-center.png)

Community Health Resource & Learning Center

Establish a center for training CHVs, caregivers, and youth, offering counseling, resources, and storage for health supplies.

![EMPTY ALT](Icons/money.svg)

Income Generating Activities for CHVs

Launch small-scale projects like poultry farming, beekeeping, kitchen gardens, and production of sanitary pads to empower CHVs economically.

![EMPTY ALT](Icons/digital-health.png)

Digital Health & Data Innovations

Develop a mobile health reporting app for real-time disease tracking, referrals, and data-driven health planning, even in remote areas.

![EMPTY ALT](Icons/partnership.svg)

Stronger Partnerships with International Organizations

Collaborating with global health organizations

![EMPTY ALT](Icons/ambulance.svg)

Mobile health clinics

Launch mobile health clinics to serve remote areas.

![EMPTY ALT](Icons/expand.png)

Expansion of health education programs

Broaden outreach to schools and the youth with vital health knowledge on prevention, nutrition, and hygiene.
