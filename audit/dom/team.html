OUR TEAM

AfyaWise operates through a transparent and inclusive leadership structure designed to ensure accountability, community ownership, and effective program delivery

Governance Structure

General Assembly

The supreme decision-making body composed of all registered AfyaWise members. The General Assembly approves policies, budgets, and reports, and elects the Executive Committee.

Executive Committee

Responsible for strategic oversight and compliance. The Executive Committee includes:

  • Chairperson
  • Vice-Chairperson
  • Secretary
  • Treasurer
  • Legal, Technical, and Community Representatives
This team sets policies, approves plans, ensures legal compliance, and supervises the Secretariat.

Secretariat

The Secretariat, led by the Executive Director, is responsible for daily management and program implementation. Key team members include:

  • Program Area Manager (Health & Nutritionz)
  • Program Coordinators (Nutrition & Health Activitiesz)
  • WASH Officer
  • Mental Health and Persons Living with Disability Officer
  • MEAL Officer (Monitoring, Evaluation, Accountability & Learning)
  • Finance & Admin Officer
  • Community Liaison Officer

Community-Based Structures

AfyaWise works closely with:

  • Community Health Volunteers (CHVsz)
  • Mental Health Leaders
  • WASH Champions and Patrons
These community structures are instrumental in mobilization, delivering services, and strengthening community engagement

Co-opted Joint Coordination Committee

A team of advisors drawn from:

  • Government representatives
  • Ministry of Health (MOH)
  • Technical experts
They provide policy advice, mobilization support, and strengthen community trust in AfyaWise programs

Leadership Commitment

Our leadership reflects the core of our mission-combining professional expertise, community knowledge, and deep commitment to improving lives. This collaborative model ensures every program we deliver directly addresses community needs with professionalism and care.
