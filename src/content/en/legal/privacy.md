AfyaWise Community Based Organization ("AfyaWise", "we") runs this website. We are the data controller for the personal data described here. This policy follows Kenya's [Data Protection Act, 2019](https://www.odpc.go.ke/) and, for visitors in the European Union and United Kingdom, the GDPR.

## What we collect

- **When you write to us** (contact, proposal or volunteer forms): your name, email, phone number, organisation, country or location, and what you tell us in your message. The volunteer form also asks for your skills and availability.
- **When you donate**: card payments are handled by Paystack and M-Pesa payments by Safaricom. Your card details never reach our website. We receive your name or phone number, email, amount, date and payment reference.
- **When you visit**: our hosting provider keeps short-lived server logs (IP address, browser, pages requested) for security. To limit spam we store a one-way code made from your IP address, never the address itself, for one hour.

Please don't send health information about yourself or others through the website. We don't need it to reply.

## Why we use it

| Purpose | Legal basis |
|---|---|
| Replying to your message or proposal request | Your consent, given in the form |
| Considering you as a volunteer | Your consent, given in the form |
| Processing and recording donations | Performing the donation, and our legal duty to keep financial records |
| Keeping the website secure and stopping spam | Our legitimate interest in a safe website |

We never sell personal data or use it for advertising.

## Who we share it with

Only service providers who help us run the website, under contract and only for these purposes:

- **Vercel Inc.** (hosting; United States)
- **Our email provider** (delivers form messages to our inboxes)
- **Paystack** and **Safaricom PLC** (donation payments, when you choose those methods)
- **Plausible Analytics** (cookieless visit statistics, if switched on; no personal profiles)
- **Chatbase** (only if you start the website chat; please don't share personal information there)

Some providers are outside Kenya. Where data leaves Kenya we rely on the safeguards the Data Protection Act requires, such as contractual protections.

## How long we keep it

- Messages and proposal requests: up to 24 months after our last contact with you.
- Volunteer applications: up to 12 months, or longer if you volunteer with us.
- Donation records: as long as Kenyan law requires for financial records.
- Server logs: kept by our host for a short period for security.

## Your rights

You can ask us to tell you what data we hold about you, correct it, delete it, or stop using it, and you can withdraw consent at any time. Email [info@afyawise.org](mailto:info@afyawise.org) and we will reply within the time the law allows.

If you are unhappy with our response, you can complain to the [Office of the Data Protection Commissioner](https://www.odpc.go.ke/). Visitors in the EU or UK can also contact their local data protection authority.

## Cookies

This website does not set advertising or tracking cookies. Payment pages run by Paystack or Safaricom, and the optional chat, may set their own cookies under their own policies.

## Children

This website is for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children through it.

## Security

The website is served over HTTPS only. Form messages go straight to AfyaWise inboxes, and only the staff who need them can see them.

## Changes

We will update this page if our practices change, and change the date below.
